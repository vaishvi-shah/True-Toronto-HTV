ⓘ Methodology & data sources

  • Rents: CMHC Rental Market Report, Toronto CMA zones, Oct 2024.
  • Transit: TTC GTFS morning-rush timetables; driving via OpenRouteService matrix.
  • Car cost: CAA 2024 — $1.62/L, 8.8 L/100 km, $410/mo fixed + workplace parking.
  • Time value: 50% of gross hourly wage over 1,950 hrs/yr (Metrolinx standard).
  • Carbon: ECCC factors — car 0.192, transit 0.032 kg CO₂e/km.
  • Climate & displacement: TRCA flood mapping, City canopy study, CMHC turnover gaps.

⛨ Tenant rights & FMTA advocacy

Ontario 2024 rent guideline: 2.5%. Above-guideline increases need LTB approval. Renoviction? You have the right to challenge a bad-faith N12/N13 at the Landlord and Tenant Board.

Help: FMTA Tenant Hotline (416) 921-9494 · Legal Aid Ontario 1-800-668-8258 · Call 211 for emergency housing.
